# 8-Week Tone-Up Plan

For Melinda Grace — home, indoor, bodyweight only. No heavy lifting, no walking or running.

**Honest note:** Regular strength work can help muscles feel firmer and stronger. Loose skin may not fully go away from exercise alone. Go at your own pace. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or joint discomfort. This is not medical advice.

## How the week works

Three short strength sessions (about 15–20 minutes each), plus gentle stretch or rest on other days.

| Day | Focus |
|-----|--------|
| Monday | Strength — thighs & glutes |
| Tuesday | Gentle stretch / mobility |
| Wednesday | Strength — core & arms |
| Thursday | Gentle stretch / mobility |
| Friday | Strength — full gentle mix |
| Saturday | Optional light stretch or rest |
| Sunday | Rest |

Each strength day: 4–5 moves, 8–12 slow reps, 2 sets, rest built into the timer. Modifications keep knees and wrists friendly.

## Weeks 1–2 — Get comfortable

Learn the moves. Use the easier options (knee wall push-ups, shorter wall sits, smaller ranges). Focus on slow, steady breathing. Checking off sessions matters more than perfection.

## Weeks 3–4 — Settle into a rhythm

Same moves, a bit more control. Hold wall sits a few seconds longer if it feels okay. Keep reps slow. Still only three strength days.

## Weeks 5–6 — Add a little challenge

If ready: fuller range on bridges and leg lifts, or an extra few seconds on holds. Skip any change that hurts. Chair dips only with a sturdy chair; otherwise stay with wall push-ups and arm circles.

## Weeks 7–8 — Keep showing up

Stay consistent. You can repeat the Friday full mix on an extra day if you feel good, or stick to three days. Use Progress to see your checkmarks. Celebrate finishing the eight weeks — then keep going at a pace that feels kind.

## Rest days

Rest is part of the plan. Stretch days are optional and gentle — no forcing. If you miss a day, pick up with Today whenever you return. There is no fail state.
